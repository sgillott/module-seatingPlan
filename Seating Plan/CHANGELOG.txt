Seating Plan
============
Graphical classroom seating plans for Gibbon.


v0.17.01
--------
Fixed
  - Printing a seating plan could put stray pieces of furniture outside the
    room, and drag a second, near-empty sheet out of the printer. Furniture
    standing against a far wall was being laid out four pixels past the room
    it belongs to, because the room's own border was being taken out of its
    floor rather than drawn around it. On screen this never showed, since
    the room sits in a frame that hides it. The room now prints as itself
    and clips anything that reaches past its walls.
  - A printed room can no longer come out larger than the paper. The size is
    now capped in millimetres as well as being worked out from the page,
    because browsers disagree about what a page measures while printing.


v0.17.00
--------
Added
  - A Print button on the seating plan. It prints the room on its own, with
    no toolbar or panels, and turns the paper to match the room: landscape
    for a room wider than it is deep, portrait for one that is deeper than
    it is wide. The room is sized to fill the page rather than to match the
    screen it was drawn on. Colleagues viewing a shared plan can print it
    too.


v0.16.00
--------
Added
  - Double-click a student in the seating plan to open their student profile.
    Teachers who cannot open student profiles get nothing, rather than being
    sent to an error page. A single click still just selects the student, and
    if the plan has unsaved changes the browser asks before leaving.


v0.15.02
--------
Fixed
  - Where two classes share a room for the same period, the register only
    loaded the marks of one of them. Students of the other class came back
    with no mark and a red border, as though their register had never been
    taken, and the next save would have written it again. Each class in a
    room has its own period in the timetable, and the register was looking
    for every mark under a single one of them. The marks themselves were
    always saved correctly.


v0.15.01
--------
Fixed
  - Confetti appeared in the top left corner of the room instead of behind
    the chosen student. The canvas it is drawn on was only 300 by 150 and
    sat in the corner, because a canvas keeps its own built-in size unless
    the stylesheet asks for a different one outright. It now covers the
    room, and the burst is measured against the canvas rather than against
    the room around it.


v0.15.00
--------
Added
  - A bar across the top of the room fills from left to right while the name
    picker slows down, green through amber to red, so it is clear something
    is happening rather than the room having stopped.
  - Confetti bursts from behind the chosen student. It comes from a library
    loaded over the internet, so a school without that will not see it; the
    picker works the same either way.

Fixed
  - The chosen student in the picker could be covered by a neighbouring
    student, who showed faded on top of them. The chosen student is now
    drawn above everything else in the room.

Note
  - The confetti library is the module's only external file. It is pinned to
    one version and checked against a hash of it, so a changed file will not
    run. Nothing else in the module needs the internet.


v0.14.01
--------
Fixed
  - Students would not sit down unless you dropped them exactly on the
    chair. A tenth of a square out and they sprang back to where they came
    from. Chairs stand side by side in a real room, so a tile even slightly
    off centre lapped over the next chair along and the drop was treated as
    landing between the two. A student now sits on whichever chair the tile
    covers most of, and only a drop squarely in the gap between two chairs
    goes back.
  - When a drop did go back, the message saying why was wiped off the status
    bar straight away and you were left with "Unsaved changes" instead.


v0.14.00
--------
Added
  - You no longer have to draw a room before you can use it. Open a lesson
    from My Lessons and Seating, Register, Picker, Rewards and Room Exits
    are all there straight away, even if nobody has ever drawn that room.
  - In a room with no chairs, put the students wherever they sit and press
    Save. The positions are kept, so you can build a seating plan first and
    draw the furniture later, or never draw it at all.
  - When you do draw the chairs afterwards, everyone moves onto the chair
    nearest to where they were standing, so the arrangement you made is not
    thrown away.
  - The layout itself is created by that first save, owned by you and shared
    with your colleagues, the same as one you add by hand.

Fixed
  - Rewards, Room Exits and the Picker used to leave out anybody the seating
    plan did not place, so a student who joined the class after the plan was
    saved could not be given a point or signed out of the room. Everybody in
    the class is now shown; anyone without a seat is laid out in rows.
  - Saving furniture in a room that had no layout used to fail with "the
    specified record cannot be found", which left the whole screen useless.


v0.13.00
--------
Added
  - Right-click a student in Rewards mode to take a point back off them.
    It removes one of whichever type is armed, the mirror of a left-click.
    The count stops at zero.
  - The status bar now says which button is armed and what a click does,
    and keeps saying it, rather than showing a message once and clearing.
  - Administrators can correct or delete a student's record for a lesson,
    from that student's page under Reports. Reward and sanction counts can
    be changed; a room exit's times can be changed, and clearing the time
    back reopens it. Every change is written to the system log with the
    old and new values.
  - The above needs the new "Manage Records" permission, which nobody has
    except administrators until you grant it.

Changed
  - Taking a point back never removes a Behaviour record, and putting the
    point back never writes a second one. The module now counts the
    records it has written rather than watching for the threshold being
    crossed, so the same crossing cannot be written twice.

Fixed
  - A fresh install could not create the room layout table. It had lost
    its primary key line, which MySQL rejects outright on a column marked
    auto_increment. Existing installations were unaffected.

Database
  - seatingPlanReward gains positiveLogged and negativeLogged, both
    starting at zero.


v0.12.04
--------
Changed
  - The picker now works through the class instead of picking at random.
    Everyone in a group gets asked before anyone is asked a second time,
    and the same student is never chosen twice running. It used to pick at
    random each time, so it would happily land on the same child four
    times while somebody else was never asked.
  - Ability bands are now thirds of the room rather than thirds of the
    students who have a CAT score. With only a few scores on file, the
    left and right bands could contain a single student, so tapping there
    always picked the same one. Students with no score rank in the middle.

v0.12.02
--------
Added
  - You can now use a room layout somebody else drew, change it, and save
    it as your own. Your save goes to a new copy. Their layout is left
    alone.
  - When the person you copied from changes their layout, your copy is
    tagged "Newer version available" in Room Layouts, and the room says so
    when you open it. You can take their version or keep yours.
  - Before you decide, both rooms are drawn side by side. Furniture you
    would lose is outlined in red, furniture you would gain in green.
  - Taking their version replaces your furniture but keeps your layout,
    its name and its seating plans. Students already seated move to the
    nearest free chair. Anyone with no chair left is unseated.

Changed

    updated one anyone has drawn for that room. It used to pick whichever
    sorted first by name, which could be a stale one.
  - Duplicating a layout records what it was copied from, so duplicates
    get the same update prompt.

Fixed
  - Saving a seating plan failed with "You do not have access to this
    action". Class lists are stored without leading zeros, and the checks
    that read them were still matching against the padded form.
  - Saving a seating plan also hit a fatal error from a missing import.
  - Seating and Register mode would not open at all. The Markbook adapter
    asked for a Gibbon\Core object in its constructor, which cannot be
    built automatically, and that took out everything depending on it.
  - The 0.12.01 database migration failed part-way on MySQL when two
    seating plans shared a class set and a student. It now merges them.

Database
  - seatingPlanRoomLayout gains seatingPlanRoomLayoutIDSource and
    sourceTimestamp, both empty for existing layouts.


v0.11.00
--------
Added
  - Room Layouts gains an Import & Export page. Export any layouts you can
    see - your own, and any a colleague has shared - as a single JSON
    file. Import brings them back, into a room you choose.
  - An exported file holds the room size, the furniture, and the names of
    the layout and its room. It holds no owner, no students, no seating
    plans and no register: there is nothing in it that identifies anyone,
    so it contains no identifying information.
  - Importing always creates a new layout belonging to you. It never
    changes or replaces one that is already there, so importing the same
    file twice gives two copies rather than overwriting the first.
    The import cannot modify an existing layout.
  - Import validates furniture types and room bounds before writing.
    Invalid furniture is reported and no empty layout is created.
    Files from newer versions of the module are rejected.


  - The room in the file is remembered by name only, which means nothing
    in another installation, so the room is always chosen on the import
    form. That is what makes a layout portable between schools.

Database
  - No new tables. The two new pages are covered by the existing Room
    Layouts action, whose URLList an installed copy is updated to include.


v0.10.00
--------
Added
  - Settings menu. Badge Settings moves into it, alongside a new
    Behaviour Settings page.
  - Behaviour Settings decides whether rewards and sanctions are also
    written into the Behaviour module. Off by default, so a quick tap
    during a lesson does not become a permanent behaviour record unless
    the school asks for it. When on: a threshold (how many points of one
    type in one lesson before the first record is written, default 3),
    an interval (one more record every this many points after that,
    default 1, set to 0 for a single record per lesson), and the
    descriptor, level and incident text to write - descriptors and levels
    offered only where the Behaviour module itself has them turned on,
    and read from its own settings so this page can never store one
    Behaviour would reject.
  - Reports menu, covering rewards, sanctions and room exits. Both pages
    filter by date range, student, year group, form group, subject,
    class, recording teacher and room, and group by any of student,
    class, subject, year group, form group, recording teacher, room or
    date - the grouping drives both the chart and the table. Student rows
    carry a circular photo and link through to a per-student page
    showing that student's own points and exits lesson by lesson.
  - Reports is a grouped action: Reports_all sees every student,
    Reports_my only students in classes the viewer teaches plus anything
    they recorded themselves.

Changed
  - Rewards mode's corner badges now count the points given in this
    lesson, not the running total for the day. That is the number the
    Behaviour threshold is measured against, so the badge now explains
    why a record was written.
  - A reward or sanction click records into the module's own ledger
    first, always - the Behaviour record, if any, follows from it. Points
    are no longer counted by matching a comment string.
  - Rewards mode and Room Exits mode both send the lesson they are in,
    and both endpoints re-resolve it from the timetable rather than
    trusting the room and date posted to them.
  - The reward badge count comes back from the server after each click
    instead of being incremented locally, so two teachers clicking in one
    room stay in sync.
  - The Staff Dashboard tab now opens on the same day My Lessons itself
    would: today, or the most recent day that actually had lessons. It
    previously used today verbatim, so it sat empty every weekend and
    holiday while My Lessons showed Friday's.

Database
  - New table seatingPlanReward: one row per student, lesson and
    recording teacher, with a running count of rewards and sanctions.
  - seatingPlanRoomExit gains gibbonCourseClassID, gibbonTTColumnRowID
    and gibbonSchoolYearID, so an exit can be reported by class, subject
    and period rather than only by room and time. Rows written before
    this version keep 0, which reads as "not attributable" - the same
    meaning it has for a student in more than one of a room's co-taught
    classes.
  - Nine new gibbonSetting rows under the Seating Plan scope.
  - Badge Settings moves from the Plans action category to Settings, and
    the Behaviour Settings and Reports actions are added.

v0.5.00
-------
Changed
  - Register mode no longer saves on every click. Cycling a student's mark
    only changes it locally; nothing is written to core's attendance tables
    until "Save Attendance" is pressed. This matches how core's own Take
    Attendance page already works (one form, one Submit for the whole
    class), and fixes a real problem with the old click-to-save design: a
    mis-click that took three cycles to correct wrote three permanent rows
    for what was really one mark.
  - Each tile's own border now shows red or green: red means today's exact
    period has no real record for that student yet, green means it does.
    This is deliberately not the same as "did the teacher touch this tile" -
    a student whose tile shows a mark only because it was carried forward
    from an earlier period (core's own crossfill/wildcard rule) starts red
    too, and is included in the very next save, because nothing has
    actually been recorded for *today's* period for them yet. A save only
    ever writes students who are red; an already-green tile left alone is
    never resubmitted.
  - The Save Attendance button is disabled until there is something to
    save, and enabled the instant a tile goes red - including on page load,
    if any student's tile is showing a crossfilled mark that still needs
    recording for today.
  - register_saveAjax.php now accepts a batch of marks in one request
    instead of one mark per request, matching seating_saveAjax.php's own
    existing all-or-nothing batch shape. One invalid mark in a save (e.g. a
    code deactivated after the page loaded) rejects the whole save with
    nothing written, rather than writing some marks and silently dropping
    others.

Database
  - None.

  - RoomContext resolves either a layout or a timetabled period into the same

mirrored and saved exactly as before.

v0.1.02
-------
Finer grid, and desks sized like desks.

Changed
  - Furniture now moves in tenths of a square instead of whole squares. A
    square is still the size of a chair; the tenths let a chair be tucked
    part-way under a desk, or a row nudged straight, instead of jumping a whole
    chair width at a time. Hold Shift while dragging to snap to whole squares.
  - The floor shows a faint dot for every tenth. The dots strengthen while you

still renders identically at any screen size.

v0.1.00
-------
Phase 1: room designer.

Added
  - Room Layouts: create, list, duplicate, share and delete furniture layouts,
    one per room per teacher with no fixed limit.
  - Full-screen designer with a ten-piece furniture palette: chair, single desk,
    double desk, teacher desk, benching, computer, board, display screen, door
    and cupboard.
  - Grid snapping, click to turn in quarter steps, drag-handle resizing for
    benching, boards and cupboards.
  - Keyboard support: Delete removes, R turns, Ctrl+S saves, Escape deselects.

    furniture catalogue and the room bounds, written in a transaction.
  - Layouts are shared with colleagues by default: readable and copyable by
    anyone teaching in that room, editable only by their owner. A duplicate
    keeps the sharing of the layout it came from.

Notes
  - Positions are stored in whole grid cells, never pixels, so a layout renders
    correctly at any screen size.
  - Seating plans and the register arrive in v0.2.00.
